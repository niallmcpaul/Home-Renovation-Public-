## D: Main Structure

### D1 Chimney stacks

**Condition Rating: 2**

The property has two rendered chimney stacks at roof level, serving living room and kitchen fireplaces. The pointing to the stack is showing signs of spalling and deterioration, particularly on the north-facing chimney. Repointing of the chimney stacks is recommended as a priority maintenance item to prevent water ingress into the flue cavity.

### D2 Roof coverings

**Condition Rating: 2**

The roof is covered with slate tiles of apparently traditional Scottish slate. Several slates on the rear slope are noted as slipped or lifted, particularly towards the ridge line. Whilst the majority of the roof covering appears serviceable, we recommend a specialist roofer inspect the slipped slates and carry out any necessary remedial work to ensure weathertightness is maintained.

### D3 Rainwater pipes and gutters

**Condition Rating: 2**

The rainwater system comprises cast-iron gutters and downpipes, original to the property. The gutters are showing evidence of deterioration with minor leaks noted at joint connections on the east elevation. The cast-iron material has a finite lifespan and replacement or repair should be planned within the next 5-10 years as a maintenance consideration.

### D4 Main walls

**Condition Rating: 3**

The external walls are solid brick construction, typical of the Victorian period. There is evidence of rising damp to the base of the rear wall, reaching approximately 1 metre up from ground level. This is caused by bridged damp proof course due to raised external ground levels around the rear garden area. The internal decorations and plaster are affected. Urgent remedial work is required: consideration should be given to lowering external ground levels and/or installing chemical damp treatment or new damp barrier.

### D5 Windows

**Condition Rating: 2**

The property retains original timber sash windows throughout. The sills to several windows, particularly on the south-west facing elevation, show signs of wet rot and paint failure. Repainting and localised timber repairs are required to several windows. The windows are generally in working order but would benefit from draught-sealing and maintenance of the sash cords and pulleys.

### D6 Outside doors

**Condition Rating: 2**

The front entrance door is a timber panelled door of appropriate period character, though the paintwork is weathered. The rear door to the kitchen is a later replacement, uPVC construction. Both doors are in serviceable condition with adequate locks and hinges, though the front door would benefit from repainting and the weathersealing inspected.

## E: Secondary Elements

### E1 Roof structure

**Condition Rating: 1**

The roof structure comprises traditional cut rafters with collar ties and purlins, visible in the accessible attic space. The timbers appear sound with no evidence of woodworm or structural distress. The structure is generally of robust design typical of late Victorian construction and is in satisfactory condition.

### E2 Ceilings

**Condition Rating: 2**

The ceilings in the reception rooms are of lath-and-plaster construction, original to the property. Fine hairline cracking is evident in several plaster ceilings, particularly in the front reception room and upper landing. This is typical minor movement cracking in properties of this age. No urgent work is required, though spot repairs to cracked plaster may be carried out for aesthetic purposes.

### E3 Walls and partitions

**Condition Rating: 1**

The internal walls are of brick or lath-and-plaster studding, typical of the period. The party wall structure appears sound. One internal partition wall on the first floor shows no significant signs of movement or structural concern. The walls provide adequate separation and structural integrity.

### E4 Floors

**Condition Rating: 2**

The ground floor comprises suspended timber boarding over brick piers and soil. There is restricted air flow through the sub-floor void, particularly under the rear extension, due to partially blocked air bricks. This restricted ventilation could lead to accumulation of moisture and potential timber decay risk. Clearing or installing additional air ventilation is recommended to ensure adequate under-floor air circulation.

### E5 Fireplaces

**Condition Rating: 1**

Two fireplaces are present: the living room has a cast-iron fireplace with tiled hearth, and the kitchen has been fitted with a timber mantle surround. Both are in serviceable condition. The chimneys serving these fireplaces require attention to pointing (see D1) but the fireplace installations themselves are in reasonable order.

## F: Services

### F1 Electricity

**Condition Rating: 3**

The electrical installation is original to the property and has not been substantially upgraded. Testing has not been carried out, but the age and nature of the installation (greater than 25 years old, with evidence of fuses rather than modern circuit breakers) indicates that an Electrical Installation Condition Report (EICR) is essential. We understand there is no Residual Current Device (RCD) protection fitted to the circuit. A full assessment by a qualified electrician registered with NICEIC or equivalent is strongly recommended prior to purchase. **This is a safety matter requiring urgent specialist attention.**

### F2 Gas/oil heating

**Condition Rating: 3**

The property is served by a cast-iron gas boiler believed to be approximately 15-20 years old. No recent service records have been provided. Given the age and lack of evidence of maintenance, we recommend an urgent inspection by a Gas Safe registered engineer to confirm safety and efficiency. The flues and ventilation serving the boiler should also be inspected. **This is a safety matter and must be addressed.**

### F4 Heating and hot water

**Condition Rating: 2**

The heating system is fed from the gas boiler noted above. Radiators throughout the property are a mix of original cast-iron and modern types, and these appear serviceable. The system provides adequate heat distribution, though the boiler's age and service history require attention as noted in F2.

### F6 Drainage

**Condition Rating: 2**

The foul drainage from the property is understood to connect to public sewer. The garage wing has a separate drainage run beneath which should be investigated further by CCTV survey to confirm the condition and check for any fractures or blockages. Surface water is managed via the rainwater goods and ground soakage. We recommend a CCTV survey of the drainage run to the garage as an advisory measure.

## G: Grounds and garage

**Condition Rating: 3**

The single-storey garage structure to the side of the property is believed to be post-WWII construction with a roof comprising asbestos cement sheets (suspected, not confirmed by testing). **Asbestos-containing materials must not be disturbed.** The garage is in otherwise fair condition. The render to a bay window section at the front of the main house shows horizontal cracking and has been previously patched with cement mortar. Localised repointing and repair is recommended. The grounds comprise paved forecourt and modest rear garden. The raised ground levels at the rear contribute to the damp issue noted under D4.

## Recommendations for Further Investigation

1. **URGENT - Electrical Safety:** Commission an Electrical Installation Condition Report (EICR) from a NICEIC-registered electrician. Determine fitness for habitation and scope for remedial works.

2. **URGENT - Gas Safety:** Arrange a Gas Safe inspection of boiler and heating system. Confirm date of last service and obtain confirmation of safety compliance.

3. **URGENT - Asbestos:** The garage roof material should be presumed to contain asbestos. Do not disturb. If disturbance is necessary (future renovation), engage a licensed asbestos contractor and follow removal protocols.

4. **Rising Damp (Priority):** Obtain specialist damp survey and remedial quotation. Consider lowering external ground levels and/or chemical injection or membrane installation.

5. **Chimney Pointing:** Repoint chimney stacks, particularly north-facing chimney, to RICS standards.

6. **Roof Maintenance:** Arrange for qualified roofer to inspect and reset slipped slates and verify weathertightness.

7. **Rainwater System:** Plan replacement or substantial repair of cast-iron gutters and downpipes within 5-10 years; address current leaks.

8. **Window Repairs:** Carry out timber repairs and repainting to sash windows, particularly sills showing rot.

9. **Sub-floor Ventilation:** Clear or install additional air bricks to improve under-floor circulation and reduce moisture risk.

10. **Drainage Survey:** Commission CCTV survey of drainage run to garage to confirm integrity.

11. **Render Repairs:** Arrange repointing and repair to bay window render cracking.

12. **Boiler Efficiency:** Once safety confirmed, consider replacement with modern condensing boiler to improve energy efficiency and running costs.
